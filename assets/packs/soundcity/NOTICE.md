# Sound City Reading alphabet sounds

Recorded by **Kathryn J. Davis**, soundcityreading.net. Used with her written permission, granted by
email on 19 September 2026:

> Yes, I give you permission to use the alphabet sounds from my website in the app you are creating.
> You may share the app as long as you give me credit, as you indicated.

Credit appears in the app on the Sounds screen, from the pack stamp in `public/sounds/manifest.json`,
and in the README. That credit is the condition of the permission: do not remove it, and do not ship
this pack without it.

Each file here is her original recording, renamed to the sound the app calls it
(`pack.json` records which of her files became which clip). Everything after that is reproducible by
`npm run sounds:build soundcity`: trim the silence, match the loudness, and run the same phonetic
gates as every other pack.
